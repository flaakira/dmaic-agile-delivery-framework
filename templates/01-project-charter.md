## Problem statement
_What is wrong, where, since when, and how big is it? Use numbers, not opinions._
> Example: "X% of weekly reports are submitted late, which adds about N hours of rework per week."

## Goal statement (SMART)
_Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound._
> Example: "Reduce late submissions from X% to Y% by [date]."
