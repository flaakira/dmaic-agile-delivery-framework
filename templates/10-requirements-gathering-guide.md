## Interview questions
**Context**
1. Walk me through how you do this task today, step by step.
2. How often do you do it, and how long does it take?
3. Which systems, files or forms do you use?

**Pain points**
4. Where do you get stuck, wait or redo work?
5. What happens when something goes wrong? Who fixes it?
6. If you could change one thing, what would it be?

**Outcomes**
7. What would "good" look like? How would you know it worked?
8. Which numbers or reports would you check?

**Constraints**
9. Who else is affected or needs to approve?
10. Are there rules, deadlines or data you can't change?
